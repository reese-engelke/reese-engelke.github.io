AXI-Stream Convolution Core

A parameterized AXI-Stream IP that loads an N×N kernel, then a matching sample window, multiplies in place, and streams the accumulated result with valid/ready/last handshaking.

  • Generic kernel size (default 3×3)
  • Separate slave and master AXI-Stream ports
  • Simulated in Vivado against a streaming testbench
